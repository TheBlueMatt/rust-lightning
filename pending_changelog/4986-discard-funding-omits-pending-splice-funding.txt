# Bug Fixes

 * When `ChannelManager::funding_contributed` rejects a contribution because the channel or peer
   is unknown or the channel is not yet funded, the `Event::DiscardFunding` it emits no longer
   lists inputs and outputs still committed to a splice that was pending when the contribution's
   `FundingTemplate` was created. Previously, a fee bump built with
   `FundingTemplate::rbf_prior_contribution` that was rejected because the channel had since
   closed reported the pending splice's inputs as discardable while that splice could still
   confirm, inviting a wallet to double-spend its own splice (#4986).
